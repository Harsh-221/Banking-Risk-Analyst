# Transaction Monitoring Policy (Synthetic Learning Document)

> This is an invented training document for the project. It is not a real bank policy or legal guidance.

## Section 1 — Alert review

Review a transaction when it differs materially from the customer's established pattern. Relevant signals can include amount outside the customer's usual range, transaction activity at an unusual hour, an unfamiliar device or location, a recently added beneficiary, and a sharp increase in transaction frequency. Consider the customer's history and the combination of signals; a single signal does not establish fraud.

## Section 2 — Analyst actions

Check the customer's recent transaction history, account authentication events, device history, beneficiary creation time, and prior alerts. Use approved customer contact details and authentication procedures to verify activity. Record the evidence reviewed and the outcome in the case record. Escalate unresolved or suspicious activity through the institution's approved process.

## Section 3 — Customer impact

An automated risk alert is a request for review. Do not describe a model score as proof of fraud. Follow the institution's authorization, hold, and customer-contact procedures; a prototype score alone must not trigger an adverse customer action.
