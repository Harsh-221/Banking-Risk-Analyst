# AML Escalation Reference (Synthetic Learning Document)

> This is an invented training document for the project. It is not a real bank policy or legal guidance.

## Section 1 — When to escalate

Escalate a case when the transaction pattern or supporting evidence remains suspicious after review, when linked activity suggests movement through multiple accounts, or when the activity meets the institution's internal escalation criteria. Consider the customer's profile, expected activity, transaction purpose, counterparties, and related transactions.

## Section 2 — Confidentiality

Use approved internal channels for escalation and limit case details to staff with a business need to know. Follow the institution's procedures on customer communications and confidentiality. Do not tell a customer that an internal suspicious-activity escalation or report has been made.

## Section 3 — No automatic conclusion

A large transaction, unusual hour, unfamiliar location, or new beneficiary alone does not establish money laundering or other wrongdoing. Document the context reviewed and follow the institution's approved escalation and reporting process.
