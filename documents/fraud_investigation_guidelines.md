# Fraud Investigation Guidelines (Synthetic Learning Document)

> This is an invented training document for the project. It is not a real bank policy or legal guidance.

## Section 1 — Account takeover indicators

For a possible account takeover, review recent login and authentication events, device enrollment, password or contact-detail changes, unusual location changes, and activity immediately before and after the alert. Compare the event sequence with the customer's baseline. Treat a new device or location as a signal to investigate, not as proof of account compromise.

## Section 2 — Payment and beneficiary checks

For a payment to a new beneficiary, review when the beneficiary was added, which authentication steps were completed, and whether other recent changes or unusual payments occurred. Confirm the payment details through approved, independently sourced customer contact channels. Do not rely on contact information supplied in a suspicious message or transaction note.

## Section 3 — Evidence and case notes

Record the alert reason, timestamps, relevant account events, customer verification outcome, evidence sources, and investigation decision. Preserve records according to the institution's retention and access-control procedures. Escalate cases that remain unresolved or meet internal escalation criteria.
